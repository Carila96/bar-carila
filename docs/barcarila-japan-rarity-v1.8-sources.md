## Evidence policy

One current menu proves active orderability, not national prevalence. Higher availability requires either cross-venue confirmation or simple/common ingredients plus broad menu evidence. Specialist-only evidence, special stock, egg/cream, fresh ingredients, long prep, or unusual base spirits lower availability and/or confidence.
